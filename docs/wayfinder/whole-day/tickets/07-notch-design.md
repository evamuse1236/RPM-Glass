Type: wayfinder:prototype (HITL) · Stream: notch · Status: open · Assignee: claude (designing 2026-10-18) · Blocked by: 06-notch-approach-prototype

## Question

States (collapsed, alert, expanded), colours per kind of change, the symbol set, motion, and tap vs long-press, drawing on Apple's Dynamic Island rules ([notch-apple-design](../research/notch-apple-design.md)). Uses `frontend-design` + `good-css`.

## Prototype (2026-10-18)

Open [prototypes/notch-options.html](../prototypes/notch-options.html) in Chrome. It shows three directions side by side on mock phones: A Quiet ink (recommended), B Living status (a ring around the camera), C Ticker. Chips at the top switch the state: Idle, Empty, Water, Routine, Meal, Expanded, Meal log, Agent toast. Plan: [notch-design-plan.md](../prototypes/notch-design-plan.md).

To close: pick A, B or C (or a mix).

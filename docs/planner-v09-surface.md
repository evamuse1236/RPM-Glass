# Full-screen planner — unified controls

Mode: Operate. Existing Android WebView/native storage. User-pinned correction to 0.8, not a replacement visual identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One plan, four scales, two ways to act. Capture AI and direct manipulation share commands; outcome blocks are an independent level between date and projects.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Frosted Night navy, pale readable text, cyan selection, bundled Jakarta/Space, solid full-screen surfaces and restrained vector controls. Glass remains specific to capture.

STORY: See the schedule immediately, tick an action, move an action to another outcome, rise from RPM to its project and life goals, or ask capture to make the same change with the active context.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Content starts at the top inset with no app-title or date-header stack. A five-destination bottom bar contains the selected date, RPM, Proj, Life and Settings. Day calendar/list controls and other infrequent actions sit within content or its action menu. A small capture/add action cluster stays above navigation.

FORM: Explicit user specification: date → RPM → projects → life → settings. The RPM overview displays labeled block drop zones, numbered handles and visible completion. Project summaries open their blocks. Horizontal swipes retain day/project movement; bottom-bar gestures offer scale movement. No concept tournament or comp: native code-led correction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Preserve scroll positions, 48dp hit areas, system Back, keyboard-safe editing, readable 200% text and reduced-motion handling. No new raster assets. Independent review must include the user's five rejected gaps and verify exact affected journeys, not just screen existence.

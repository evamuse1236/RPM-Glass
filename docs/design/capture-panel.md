# Design: Capture panel

Part of [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). 

The full behaviour is in [PRODUCT.md](../product/capture.md). Visually, the panel has a header (a monochrome `edit_note` glyph in a primary-container circle, the title, Expand, More and Close, with Expand drawn no heavier than Close) and a content area that shows one current state at a time: empty, listening, saving, saved and sorting, proposals, one question, or a receipt with Undo. More drops its menu from the header, as Android's overflow does. At the bottom is one filled pill composer in every state, holding only the words and, at its end, the microphone, which becomes a filled Send button once there is text and stays on the last line as the words grow. The field has no outline or focus ring in any state; the caret and the keyboard show focus, as in Gemini. The empty state is the field alone with the one trust line under it; the planning starters live in More. Sorting shows one line, "Your words are saved", that opens to the exact words, then a visible indeterminate progress line and one plain sentence ("Sorting into your plan… You can close this."), no skeletons. Proposals use a single border level: one outlined list with a divider between rows. Every row has one structure: an eyebrow on changes, the title (body-large) with the Must star at its end only on a Must and a quiet close button ("Leave out") last, a "Was …" line under a renamed saved task, then one wrapping row of 8dp-corner outlined assist chips (day, Block or Inbox, estimate, reminder, repeat) with primary icons. A changed day reads "from → to" inside its chip; an assistant-suggested day has a dashed outline and a "Suggested" tag in label-medium. Chips wrap rather than truncate. Warnings are body-medium lines in the error colour under the chips. A question sits in a `surface-container-high` box inside its row, title-small prompt over 40dp outlined answer chips; it never uses the error colour. Also heard follows the list: a quiet header ("Also heard · kept with your words in History") and one row per note with a monochrome icon (lightbulb for an idea, a tick for something already saved) and, for ideas and intentions, an Add to Inbox text button. No leading checkboxes or ticks on proposals, which would read as done tasks. A left-out row keeps its place, fades its lines and offers Put back. There are no Area dots on proposals or in the Block picker. The receipt is not a copy of the proposals: a title-medium count ("2 tasks added") and one line per task (the title with its Must star, then "Sun 4 Oct, 9:00 AM · DAD Excel workbook submitted" in the variant colour, wrapping between phrases), with no ticks or containers. Within one capture the panel only grows, so the composer and the main action keep their place; the receipt settles to its own height.

## Composer layout

A dark composer floating over whatever app is open, dark in both themes. It sits 16dp from the sides and 16dp above the keyboard or the screen's bottom edge, with 36dp corners, a 1px `--bar-outline` border, `--bar-shadow` (36dp is the scale's step nearest the mockup's 32). The composer is one row: the field, then one 48dp round button, with 8dp around the button and 20dp before the words. The field is body-large (16/24) with 12 0 padding, at every text size; it has no outline or focus ring and grows to six lines before it scrolls, the button staying on its last line. At rest the button is the voice orb with a mic glyph (`#dictate`, "Voice input"); once the field has text it cross-fades and scales (from 60%) to Send (`#send`): a filled `--bar-primary` (#A8C7FA) circle with the `send` paper plane in `--bar-on-primary` (#062E6F), the one lit control. There is no second row and no overflow dots. The handle at the top centre is the More button (`#more`): a 48 × 24 outlined tab with an `expand_less` chevron in a 48dp target, `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls="sheet"`; a tap opens the More sheet, the chevron turns over while it is open, and a drag up on it still expands the panel. `#status` is one label line just above the box, hidden when empty; an error line may wrap. At rest the composer is 64dp tall. Round buttons press to 96%. While a ramble is live the orb is the voice: its glyph fades, it follows the level (`--level`), spins fast while listening and breathes while RPM works or speaks, and a tap on it stops and saves ("Stop and save"); it is disabled only while RPM saves or speaks. Under Reduce Motion the orb holds still. Response cards stack 8dp above the composer as separate cards with a 1px `--bar-outline` border, 20dp corners (the scale's step for the mockup's 24) and a short shadow, so card and composer read as one concentric stack. Chips have 8dp corners and lead with an 18px icon: on a field question the kind of value (schedule, timer, target), on "Which one?" what choosing does (delete, check_circle); the first "Which one?" chip, which a right swipe picks, has a primary outline and a filled icon. Expand fills the available height.

## Voice card

The live card has no orb and no Stop: the composer orb does both. It shows the streamed words (each word blurs in), then RPM's sentence while it speaks, then the spine rows, all changed in place. The receipt's header row holds the title (the tally, such as "3 changes" or "Added") and, at its end, three 40dp round buttons in 48dp targets: Discard (`undo`, filled, tonal `--bar-highest`), Edit (`edit_square`, filled, tonal) and Open planner (`arrow_outward`, filled `--bar-primary` with a `--bar-on-primary` icon). Under it come RPM's sentence (body-medium) and the spine. There is no bottom row, divider, capsule or red icon. After a discard the title is "Discarded", only Open planner stays, the sentence is "Your words are kept in History." and the rows fade and strike through. A card taller than the space scrolls inside `.voice-scroll` with the header pinned.

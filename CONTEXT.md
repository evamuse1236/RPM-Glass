# RPM planning

RPM connects desired outcomes and personal reasons to manageable actions. Capture and planning are separate activities.

## Language

**Task**: A concrete action that can be captured before it has a time or an RPM block. Its estimated duration is distinct from its scheduled start and actual time spent.

**RPM block**: A specific desired result, its personal purpose, and a flexible set of actions that can help achieve it. Not every action is a must.
_Avoid_: Time block when referring to this outcome-centered grouping.

**Project**: A larger undertaking containing RPM blocks. This hierarchy follows Dara's explicit product definition.

**Life area**: A user-defined part of life under which goals can be organized.

**Yearly goal**: An outcome for a chosen year associated with a life area and optionally linked to projects.

**Must**: An action deliberately marked as essential to the current plan, independently of its priority position.

**Priority**: The relative ordering of actions within a block; not necessarily their chronological execution order.

**Calendar commitment**: A read-only event from a selected device-synced calendar. It informs conflicts but is not an RPM task or an RPM alarm.

**Planning draft**: An AI-proposed arrangement not yet accepted by the user. Untouched drafts are not evidence of approval.

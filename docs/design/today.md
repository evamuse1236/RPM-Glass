# Design: Today

Part of [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). The approved look is the prototype `today-v3` (2026-10-20); the habit circle follows `components-v1`.

## Day layout (the default)

- **Band.** The top of the page is `surface-container`. The big date reads "20. October 2026 ›" in `--title-date` (700 28/34), with the year and chevron in `primary`; it opens the date picker. Under it, seven days from today (swiping steps a week): a short weekday over a 32dp number, today's number in a filled `primary` circle, the selected day on `surface-container-highest`. Under each number sit up to five dots in its tasks' Area tones, after the deadline hourglass and the clash mark. With large text the strip gives way, as before. The "Today" heading stays for screen readers but is visually hidden; the top bar keeps Search, Capture and More options.
- **Sheet.** A `surface` sheet with 20dp top corners overlaps the band by 12dp and holds everything below.
- **Anytime chips.** All-day events and tasks dated for the day without a time are one wrapping row of 32dp capsule chips: a 16dp icon and a 13/18 weight-500 label on `surface-container-high`, open tasks first. A chip opens its task. Water is never a chip.
- **Timeline.** A 64dp gutter of times (12/16 weight 500, `on-surface-variant`, right-aligned; the start and, for a span of 45 min or more, its end), and a 2dp rail in `surface-container-highest` with one marker per row (redesigned 2026-10-28; it replaces `today-v3`'s coloured icon pill plus a second ring). A task, Routine or meal has a 24dp check ring on the rail in a 40 × 40 target (48dp touch): the row's tone for its edge, filled in that tone with a check once done or logged. An event or deadline has a 24dp `surface-container-highest` disc with its 16dp icon (an overdue deadline's icon in `error`). Behind the marker, a 4dp tone bar as long as the item (40dp minimum, 0.72dp a minute on the 4dp grid, at most 160) sets the row's height; a moment such as a meal has none. On a tall row the ring and the title sit at the top, beside the start time. The time shows only in the gutter, and every row shows its own time, even when two share it. The words are the title (`--title-row`, 600 16/22, with the Must star; a meal 400 16/24) and at most one meta line (13/18): "in 12 min" or "Now · 20 min left" in `primary` weight 600, "Not done", "Overdue" or a clash in `error`, then the Result, a Routine's count or a logged meal, joined by " · ". An unlogged meal has no meta line; "Not logged" stays in its spoken name. Done rows grey their title and drop it to weight 400, and a done task or Routine strikes it through (a logged meal does not); past rows grey their title; both fade the bar. Meals use `--tone-meal`, Routines `--tone-routine`, tasks their Area's tone, events and deadlines `outline-variant`.
- **Free time and now.** A gap of 10 min or more reads "N free" (13/18, muted) where titles start; a gap of an hour or more that has not passed adds a 20dp + at the row's end (on the wide website, right after the words) named "Plan something in N". Now is a 2dp `primary` line from the rail with its time in the gutter in `primary`, or the row in progress.
- **Habit circle.** A 48dp glass circle (the tab bar's glass) 12dp above Add, only on Today. It shows the first due habit in `HABITS` (`habit-priority.mjs`: water, then meals) on the shared due rules, and nothing when none is due. Its outline is a segmented meter (r 22 in a 48 viewBox, 3px stroke, 3.2px gaps): water has one arc per glass of the goal, filled in `--tone-water`; a meal has one arc per meal slot that is on, logged ones in `--tone-meal`. There is no text inside. Water shows a liquid fill in an 18px circle at count/goal with a front wave and a 40% back wave and a drop that turns dark once covered; a meal shows an empty amber bowl. Tapping water logs a glass in place with the snackbar and Undo (a drop falls in, a ripple spreads, the level rises with overshoot, the waves slosh about 1.5 s, the segment draws in; `glass-animation.mjs`, swappable). Tapping a meal opens Log a meal on that slot; once it is logged, three bits pop into the bowl, two steam curls rise and the segment draws in. When the habit changes the old circle shrinks and fades (200ms) and the new one rises from Add (360ms spring). Nothing moves at rest; reduced motion makes every change instant. Names: "Water, 3 of 8 glasses. Log a glass", "Lunch not logged. Log lunch". A long press on water opens the Water sheet.

Keylines: the gutter's times end at 56 inside a 64dp column, the rail, its markers and tone bars are centred at 84 (the marker column runs 64 to 104), the words start at 116, and a free row's + ends 16dp from the edge. The gutter grows with the text scale.

## List layout

Under the date, a strip of the next seven days starting today (swiping steps a week) marks days holding Results' deadlines with an hourglass (and their count when more than one), days with a calendar clash with the clash mark in the error colour, and other days with tasks with a dot; tapping a day lists that day's deadlines and clash above its agenda. Under the strip, one quiet line says where the week's plan stands and opens the weekly review: this week's Results (only Results the user marked achieved count), or from Friday to Sunday "Week of Oct 5 isn't planned · review 7:30 PM" (the user's own timed "Weekly review" task today, which stays in the agenda at its time). Then one lead card for now, the screen's only filled button. When there is half an hour or more free before the next timed task or busy event, it is **Free 8:00 – 10:00 AM · in 1 h** (or "Free until 10:00 AM" once the gap has started): the nearest deadline (overdue first, then due by the end of the day after tomorrow) whose unscheduled tasks fit, as the header, those tasks, and "Schedule at 8:00 AM", which schedules them back to back into the gap with Undo. Otherwise it is **Do next**: a primary "Do next · in 4 h" label (or "Now · 20 min left"), the Result as a short header (title-medium, then its Purpose on its own quieter line, then one facts line: "Due tomorrow 11:00 PM · in 40 h · 5 tasks left"), then the task itself. With large text the title and Purpose wrap in full and the facts keep the deadline; the countdown, the task count, the free card's task durations and the strip give way so the first row below stays in view, and the week line moves under Coming up. **Coming up · Next 3 days** follows: every other Result due in the next three days and every calendar clash, soonest first, as plain list rows with a neutral leading icon, the headline on its own full-width line, then the supporting lines beside at most one trailing text button. A Result row says "Due tomorrow 11:59 PM · in 41 h" (the one deadline format on Today) over "2 tasks left"; one due by the end of the day after tomorrow with nothing planned before its deadline adds "not scheduled yet" and Add to today (dates its first undated task, with Undo). An overdue Result says so in the error colour. A clash (overlapping events, or a timed task and an event, in the next three days; overlaps join into one row) has "Clash · Mon 9 – 11 AM" with Resolve beside it and one line per item with its time span; a task says "can move", and the task that sets a Result's deadline shows "Due 10:30 AM" in place of its span. Resolve opens a sheet listing the overlapping items, where a task can move to just before the clash or open to change its time, both with Undo (never the deadline task in one tap), and a clash of calendar events only offers one task today to sort it out. The error colour is only for a clash or an overdue Result. Then **Later today**: one time-ordered agenda, as Calendar's schedule, with tasks (their Result on a third line) and calendar events (a tonal fixed block whose text lines up with the task titles) interleaved, the free time between them as one quiet line on a dotted rail ("2 h 30 min free"), and tasks with no time last. The Inbox row with its count ends the page. Every row's text starts at the same 56dp edge. The Timeline is a secondary view of the same day.

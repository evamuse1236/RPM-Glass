# Full-screen planner

Mode: Operate. Android phone, existing offline WebView and native storage. Preserve fast widget capture and independent settings. The user specified the composition and delegated remaining decisions overnight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Move between scales of one plan: the day timeline, projects and their outcome blocks, and life areas/yearly goals. Preserve each level's place.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Frosted Night's deep navy ground, readable pale text, cyan selections, Jakarta body and Space headings. Solid full-screen surfaces keep a dense time ruler legible; glass remains in the floating widget.

STORY: Open today, inspect commitments, resize or edit a task, rise to its project and result, then to the longer-term goal. Return without losing the day or selected project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact title/date toolbar and three labeled scale controls above a vertically scrolling hourly ruler. Timed tasks are proportional to duration, external commitments have a distinct outline, and unscheduled capture has a compact entry point. No giant summary cards.

FORM: User-pinned three-level spatial navigation, implemented directly in the existing visual system. Header swipes change levels; horizontal content swipes change day/project; task handles own drag/resize. This precisely specified form does not require a concept tournament. Code-led extension of existing components.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Motion: 180–220ms directional transitions; content is present from the start, no blur on text, no animation under reduced-motion preference. Numeric estimates use compact tabular labels and a quiet duration indicator. Accessible buttons duplicate gestures.

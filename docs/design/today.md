# Design: Today

Part of [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). The approved look is the prototype `today-v3` (2026-10-20); the habit circle follows `components-v1`.

## Day layout (the default)

- **Header.** One compact top-bar row on `surface`: the date "7 November 2026 ›" (day first, no dot, title-large, one text colour) opens the date picker, with Search and More options at its end; on another day a text-only tonal "Today" chip follows the date, clear of Search even for the longest date (`dateLine` in `planner/today-header.mjs`, added by `rootBar` in `planner/shell.mjs`). Every shown date puts the day first ("Sat 7 Nov", "Saturday 7 November"; `DATE_LOCALE` in `planner/format.mjs`). The "Today" heading stays for screen readers but is visually hidden. Under it, seven days from today (swiping steps a week): a short weekday over a 32dp number, today's number in a filled `primary` circle, the selected day on `surface-container-highest`, and at most one mark with one meaning: a 4dp `on-surface-variant` dot under another day that still holds an open Must or a Result's deadline, never on the selected day's disc, whose work shows below; today keeps its dot while another day is open (`dayMark({due, musts, key, day})`; the day's label names them for TalkBack). Nothing else goes in the strip; a clash shows in the day itself. With large text the strip gives way, as before. There is no band and no overlapping sheet.
- **Now card.** The first block under the strip is one quiet card (no filled button) holding one row: the item in progress, with its time left and a thin `primary` progress line, or else the next timed item with its countdown. When nothing timed is live or next (the evening), there is no card: an Any-time task or a missed Must is not "now", and both stay in the timeline. It never says "Ended X ago". Its gutter time ends on the same 56 keyline as every gutter time, in `on-surface-variant`, and keeps 12dp inside the card's edge ("11:30 AM" breaks before "AM" to fit; it never crosses the edge); blue is kept for the now line. The row is not repeated in the timeline.
- **Still to do, and Show done.** Right under the Now card, one group lists what is owed from earlier (`owedRows`): open tasks past their end, Musts included, and overdue deadlines, in clock order. Its header is a quiet label-large line on the page edge, "Still to do · 4" (`owedLabel`; a heading "Still to do from earlier, 4" for TalkBack), with "Swipe left to plan" in label-small at its end on touch screens. Owed rows keep the timeline's row rhythm and padding: the start time alone in the gutter, beside the first title line with the ring, no length bar, and one meta line: "2 h late" (whole hours since its end, or "20 min late"; `lateText`), then " · " and its Result, never "Missed". Only a Must is loud: its ring and "N h late" use `--error`; any other late row has an `--outline` ring and an `on-surface-variant` "N h late". An overdue deadline keeps its error disc and "Overdue". Each keeps its swipe left to schedule. Every Must shows, in clock order; the other rows fill the rest of three, so a Must is never folded (`owedSplit`). Under the group, one primary text line at the top of the rail opens everything else in place: "Show 2 more · 1 done" (the owed rows that do not fit, never a Must, then every other past row: done, logged, events, free time; "· 2 earlier" when none was done; "Show 1 done" alone when nothing owed is hidden; "Show less" when open; TalkBack "Show earlier: 3 done, 2 more"; `foldWords`, `doneLabel`, `doneSpoken`). There is never a second expander. Neither line has a ring or a disc. The Now line splits what is done from what is next. Scrolled, the top bar's `surface-container` fades into the page over 12dp, so rows slide under a soft edge. A scroll that stops near the Now card settles with the card wholly under the bar (proximity snap at the card and at the page top), so its title is never cut.
- **Anytime rows.** All-day events and tasks dated for the day without a time are ordinary timeline rows at the top, not chips. Water is never one of them.
- **Timeline.** A 64dp gutter of times (12/16 weight 500, `on-surface-variant`, right-aligned, never wrapped; for a span of 45 min or more its end sits under the start in weight 400, unless a later row starts by then), and a 2dp rail in one tone with one marker per row. A task, Routine or meal has a 24dp check ring on the rail in a 40 × 40 target (48dp touch), filled with a check once done or logged; a task that was not done keeps a hollow `--error` ring in the Still to do group. An event or deadline has a 24dp `surface-container-highest` disc with its 16dp icon (an hourglass for a deadline, with no "Due ·" prefix). Behind the marker, a 4dp tone bar as long as the item (40dp minimum, 0.72dp a minute on the 4dp grid, at most 160) sets the row's height. The time shows only in the gutter. The words are the title (`--title-row`, 600 16/22, with the Must star; a meal 400 16/24), wrapping at one width, and at most one meta line (13/18): a countdown, lateness, the Result, a Routine's count or a logged meal, joined by " · ". A task over an event that has not ended shows one inline label, "Clash · <event>" in the error colour on one line with an ellipsis, that still opens Resolve; a past task shows none. An unlogged meal has no meta line; "Not logged" stays in its spoken name. Done rows grey and strike their title; past rows grey their title.
- **Free time and now.** Free time is space on the rail, counted only inside the waking day (8 AM to 10 PM). A gap of an hour or more that has not passed reads "N free" (13/18, muted) with a 20dp + named "Plan something in N". A past or shorter gap has no words and is 16dp tall; blank space right after the Now line or after another blank gap is left out. Now is a 2dp `primary` line from the rail with its time in the gutter in `primary`.
- **Website.** The wide website keeps the same header, Now card and timeline rows. Its right column is one **Coming up** list grouped by day, with flat full-width 1px `outline-variant` dividers and no side rule. It covers the next two weeks, at most six days, skipping empty days and the day the centre shows; overdue items from the last seven days (Results and missed Musts) head it under "Overdue". Every item sits under its own day: a Must dated another day than its Result's deadline is listed under its own day, not nested under the Result. Each day is named once by its full date ("Tue 10 November"); only the real today and tomorrow add "Today ·" or "Tomorrow ·", whatever day the centre shows. Under it, each deadline is one row, ordered by time: a Result with an open Must dated that day leads with the Must (star, its title wrapping in full beside the time in its own column, then a quiet second line across the full width: the Result, ending in an ellipsis, and "· 3 tasks left" kept whole); a Result with no such Must is one hourglass row (title, "3 tasks left"); the day's other one-time Musts follow with their Result, if any, on the second line. No title is ever clipped; the second line is always one line. The wide website's gutter is the phone's: the start, and for a span of 45 min or more its end under it. An overdue item shows its time as other rows do: only the time for today, the weekday and time ("Wednesday, 9:00 AM") within the last week, the full date and time before that; the Overdue heading says it is late, so the day is never said twice. "· 5 tasks left" never wraps on its own. Only what is overdue reads in the Must amber. The Inbox stays in the left nav, not as a card (`web/app/agenda.mjs`).
- **Habit circle.** A 48dp glass circle (the tab bar's glass) 12dp above Add, only on Today. It shows the first due habit in `HABITS` (`habit-priority.mjs`: water, then meals) on the shared due rules, and nothing when none is due. Its outline is a segmented meter (r 22 in a 48 viewBox, 3px stroke, 3.2px gaps): water has one arc per glass of the goal, filled in `--tone-water`; a meal has one arc per meal slot that is on, logged ones in `--tone-meal`. There is no text inside. Water shows a liquid fill in an 18px circle at count/goal with a front wave and a 40% back wave and a drop that turns dark once covered; a meal shows an empty amber bowl. Tapping water logs a glass in place with the snackbar and Undo (a drop falls in, a ripple spreads, the level rises with overshoot, the waves slosh about 1.5 s, the segment draws in; `glass-animation.mjs`, swappable). Tapping a meal opens Log a meal on that slot; once it is logged, three bits pop into the bowl, two steam curls rise and the segment draws in. When the habit changes the old circle shrinks and fades (200ms) and the new one rises from Add (360ms spring). Nothing moves at rest; reduced motion makes every change instant. Names: "Water, 3 of 8 glasses. Log a glass", "Lunch not logged. Log lunch". A long press on water opens the Water sheet.

Keylines: the gutter's times end at 56 inside a 64dp column, the rail, its markers and tone bars are centred at 84 (the marker column runs 64 to 104), the words start at 116, and a free row's + ends 16dp from the edge. The gutter grows with the text scale.

## List layout

Under the date, a strip of the next seven days starting today (swiping steps a week) marks another day that still holds an open Must or a Result's deadline with one neutral dot, as in the day layout; tapping a day lists that day's deadlines and clash above its agenda. Under the strip, one quiet line says where the week's plan stands and opens the weekly review: this week's Results (only Results the user marked achieved count), or from Friday to Sunday "Week of Oct 5 isn't planned · review 7:30 PM" (the user's own timed "Weekly review" task today, which stays in the agenda at its time). Then one lead card for now, the screen's only filled button. When there is half an hour or more free before the next timed task or busy event, it is **Free 8:00 – 10:00 AM · in 1 h** (or "Free until 10:00 AM" once the gap has started): the nearest deadline (overdue first, then due by the end of the day after tomorrow) whose unscheduled tasks fit, as the header, those tasks, and "Schedule at 8:00 AM", which schedules them back to back into the gap with Undo. Otherwise it is **Do next**: a primary "Do next · in 4 h" label (or "Now · 20 min left"), the Result as a short header (title-medium, then its Purpose on its own quieter line, then one facts line: "Due tomorrow 11:00 PM · in 40 h · 5 tasks left"), then the task itself. With large text the title and Purpose wrap in full and the facts keep the deadline; the countdown, the task count, the free card's task durations and the strip give way so the first row below stays in view, and the week line moves under Coming up. **Coming up · Next 3 days** follows: every other Result due in the next three days and every calendar clash, soonest first, as plain list rows with a neutral leading icon, the headline on its own full-width line, then the supporting lines beside at most one trailing text button. A Result row says "Due tomorrow 11:59 PM · in 41 h" (the one deadline format on Today) over "2 tasks left"; one due by the end of the day after tomorrow with nothing planned before its deadline adds "not scheduled yet" and Add to today (dates its first undated task, with Undo). An overdue Result says so in the error colour. A clash (overlapping events, or a timed task and an event, in the next three days; overlaps join into one row) has "Clash · Mon 9 – 11 AM" with Resolve beside it and one line per item with its time span; a task says "can move", and the task that sets a Result's deadline shows "Due 10:30 AM" in place of its span. Resolve opens a sheet listing the overlapping items, where a task can move to just before the clash or open to change its time, both with Undo (never the deadline task in one tap), and a clash of calendar events only offers one task today to sort it out. The error colour is only for a clash or an overdue Result. Then **Later today**: one time-ordered agenda, as Calendar's schedule, with tasks (their Result on a third line) and calendar events (a tonal fixed block whose text lines up with the task titles) interleaved, the free time between them as one quiet line on a dotted rail ("2 h 30 min free"), and tasks with no time last. The Inbox row with its count ends the page. Every row's text starts at the same 56dp edge. The Timeline is a secondary view of the same day.

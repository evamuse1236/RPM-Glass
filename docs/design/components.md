# Design: components

Part of [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). 

- **Task row** (Google Tasks): a circular completion checkbox with a 48dp target, the title, then the time, duration and Area on one line, and, on a Must only, a filled star on the right. Non-Musts show nothing there, and the star is a mark, not a button: Must is set in the task sheet (Dara's decision, 2026-10-03), so a stray tap never changes it. A time carries its day unless it is today ("Tomorrow 9:00 PM", "Mon 9:45 AM"); a Plan row also says "Today". Must is the amber star alone (with an accessible name), never the star and the word; Block detail gives the legend once, the star beside "of Musts left". Titles wrap with `text-wrap: pretty` so a row never ends on one orphaned word. Completed rows strike through and move into a collapsed Completed group. Overdue rows say "Overdue". Task rows (Today's spine cards, Block Plan rows, the Inbox and lists) swipe: the row's surface slides with the finger over a reveal with an icon and the word. Swipe right is **Done** (check, tertiary container); swipe left is **Schedule** (event_upcoming, primary container); in the Inbox its menu ends with **Move to Block…**. A drag locks to one axis after 8px, so a vertical scroll never moves a row. The reveal shows tinted from the lock; at the 90dp commit it fills and its icon grows to 115% (40ms) with one haptic tick. Letting go short springs the row back (200ms). Done carries the row off the edge (150ms) and it collapses where it was (250ms), with Undo; on Today's spine the card springs back and ticks in place, because done tasks stay on the spine. Schedule springs back while a menu of one-tap choices opens from the row (each with its time in the variant colour, the current one checked). Delete is never a swipe: a long press (400ms) opens a quick menu with Must, Move to Block and Delete; on Block Plan rows the long press stays the reorder lift, and Delete is in the task sheet's `Task actions`. Completing fills the ring tertiary and pops it 1 → 1.2 → 1 on the spring (150ms).
- **Result group / Block card:** a tonal card with the Result (title-medium), Purpose (body-medium, variant colour), one facts line (the deadline in medium weight, the open Must time after a Must star, "2 of 7 done"; no countdown, the day says how near it is) and the next task. On the Blocks list, cards in a row from the same Project sit under one Area and Project line, as Calendar's schedule names a day once; a Project filter or the Project page already says it, so there it is left out. "Picked for this week" marks the Results chosen in the weekly review.
- **Block detail** reads like a Google Tasks detail page. Under the Area and Project line (with a drop-down arrow: it opens the Projects as a menu) and the headline-small title come icon rows, each 24dp icon centred on the Plan's check column and its text aligned with the task titles, in the order of the Block card: the Purpose (labelled), then where the Result stands by its progress ring ("Due tomorrow 11:00 PM · 41 h left" over "2 of 7 done · ★ 3 h 50 min of Musts left", the star being the Must legend; "2 of 7 tasks done" leads when there is no deadline), then "Is this Result achieved?" with the subline "Ticked tasks don't decide it" and an outlined Mark achieved button (the green achieved panel in its place once marked; both ways have Undo). The deadline line uses the error colour, with words, when overdue or tight. Then the Plan, whose header holds Sort by time (only while dated tasks are out of time order). Plan rows have no drag handle: a held row lifts (surface-container-high, elevation 3, 102% scale) and follows the finger while each row slides out of its way as soon as the card crosses its middle (150ms standard easing), then glides into its exact slot and lands flat (scale, shadow and fill, 200ms); only then does the page take the new order, with no rebuild, so nothing moves twice. The Plan header keeps Sort by time's height whether it shows or not, and the link fades in (200ms, decelerating) or out (150ms). "Add or find a task" sits inline under the Plan, and there is no FAB. Focused, it unfolds (200ms, in the page's flow) the newest three Inbox tasks; typing narrows them to Inbox tasks whose words start with the typed words (matched starts in bold, at most four, each with its day and time or "Inbox"), then a "Create …" row. Tapping a match, or Arrow keys then Enter, moves it into this Block with Undo; Enter alone creates the task. Escape folds the list, a second Escape clears the field. the field is one node for the life of the page (a save never rebuilds it), and Enter puts the typed words into the new Plan row above it in the same frame the field empties (the row at full opacity at its final place, shown from a copy of the data while the save runs, so no second re-render follows); the row's box opens from the top (200ms) while the whole add row (+, field, hint and a steady caret) slides down by exactly that room, so the two never share space and the add row is never hidden. Only the new row and the changed words touch the page; the Plan's status row keeps its node and its new words settle in. The title, Purpose and Notes edit in place: they read as plain text, with a small pencil after the Purpose and Notes labels as the resting hint (gone while editing), and a focused one gains a surface-container-high field with a 2dp primary indicator under it (150ms), with no Save button and no Edit pencil in the top bar. When the words wrap onto more or fewer lines, what follows slides to its new place (200ms), never jumps. Where the Result stands always takes two lines, so the first task added changes its words, not its height. Once the title has scrolled away, the top bar shows it with the deadline line as its subtitle. There are no chips or tonal panels above the Plan. Task completion never marks a Result achieved; only the user does.
- **Buttons:** one filled button per screen, tonal or outlined for secondary actions, and text buttons for tertiary ones. All are 40dp visually inside a 48dp target.
- **Chips:** filter chips for views and filters, assist chips for suggestions.
- **Snackbar:** an inverse surface with a single Undo action. Over an open sheet it docks just above the sheet's action bar and the sheet's content ends 12dp above it (the body's bottom margin grows by its height in 150ms, ahead of the bar rising, and the content's last 16dp fade like a scroll edge), so it never covers a live control. The task sheet keeps that room free under its rows from the moment it opens, so the bar usually arrives with nothing moving; only if the row just tapped would end up under it does the sheet grow taller (150ms), and only when it can grow no more does the body slide (250ms standard), never so far that the task's title leaves the top. A leaving sheet keeps its layout frozen, and the bar settles to its resting place with it. Every plan change made from the planner, the review or Capture can be undone.
- **Sheets:** Task detail and Quick add are bottom sheets with 36dp top corners and a 1px float border on the top and sides. In light a sheet is `surface-container-low`; in dark it is `surface-container-high` (#282A2C, the tab bar's tone) with an `outline-variant` edge, so it stands off the scrim. What sits raised on it (search field, Block row, stepper buttons, a lifted row) takes the next step up. The primary action stays above the keyboard. The task sheet stands from the bottom to just under the top bar (`calc(100dvh - 64px)`); its layout is the approved prototype `sheet-v3`, variant 2 (2026-10-20). Its header holds the task's 40dp icon pill (the title's symbol in its Area tone, as on Today), the title (`--title-row`, three lines, all of it at large text), the Must star and the completion ring (the only complete control; a right swipe past 96px on the header row does the same, the ring filling at 60%), with a `Start now` assist chip under the title (play icon in primary) that makes an open one-time task begin at this minute today, with Undo; it hides while the task runs, once it is done, and on a repeating task. The Close button is kept for TalkBack but not drawn; the handle and Back close the sheet. A tap on a task always opens the sheet at once; there is no double tap. Under the header a row of four tabs on `sheet-card` (20dp corners, 4dp in) shows each tab's icon over its value: When ("4 PM", "Wed 4 PM"), Alert ("Off", "On time", "10 min"), Repeat ("Once", "Weekdays", "Mo We Fr") and Block (the Block's first word, or Inbox). A raised 16dp pill slides to the chosen tab (anchor positioning, 250ms emphasized; reduced motion jumps) and its icon fills in primary; at large text the tabs sit two by two. When is open first. The chosen panel fades up 8dp over 200ms after a tap. **When:** seven day circles from today (a short weekday over a 40dp number; the chosen day filled `primary`, today's number in primary; a picked day outside them gets its own circle in order), then the time readout ("4 PM" in `--title-date` at 600, "to 4:45 PM" in title-small), then a 72dp ruler on `sheet-card` with 16dp corners that slides sideways under a fixed 2dp centre line and snaps every half hour from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, plus the task's own off-grid time as its own primary tick; hour labels (label-small; every other hour at large text), 4dp grey marks for busy times (other tasks and calendar events that day) and a translucent bar in the Area tone for the task's length. Letting go saves the stop under the line (a tick haptic per stop while sliding); arrow keys step it. Then length chips (15 min to 2 h, plus the task's own), then one row of Pick a date, Pick a time, Other length, No time (not for a repeating task), No date and No length. Without a day the ruler sits at 38% and does nothing. The calendar clash line sits under the ruler. **Alert:** a segmented Off / Remind / Alarm on a pill track (selected `secondary-container`), then lead chips On time, 5, 10, 30 and 60 min before, hidden while the alert is off; without a day and time the control sits at 38% with the words "An alert needs a day and a time." **Repeat:** seven 40dp circles M T W T F S S that toggle (selected `secondary-container`), saved as the plainest pattern (none, daily, weekdays, weekly on the task's own day, or chosen days), then Once, Weekdays, Every day and After completion, which asks for its number of days. **Block:** a vertical list, one 56dp row per open Block in the planner's order: its progress ring (tasks done of total, the Block card's ring, in the Area tone), the Block's title, then "Purpose · 2 of 5 done" in body-small, Recent or Suggested beside the title, and a radio mark on the right; the chosen row is tinted with its tone. The Inbox row is last. A search field shows from seven Blocks. Tapping a row moves the task there with Undo. Every choice saves at once with the snackbar and Undo; there is no Save button. Below the tabs, under a 1px rule: Why (a task with no Block), Notes and Who could help as borderless one-line fields that grow, and the original words when they differ from the title; then the footer toolbar `Task actions` (Ask Capture, Duplicate, Archive, Delete in the error colour, and Undo last completion on a completed repeat), icon over caption, at least 72dp tall. At 200% text the title takes its own full-width line under the pill, the day circles scroll sideways, chip labels stay on one line, rows still scroll, and the body scrolls between header and toolbar, whose captions wrap. Every press scales to 0.97 (100ms). Quick add opens in 300ms. Its title is 16/24 (body-large, like Capture's field). Its chips sit one row per meaning, every chip labelled, 32dp tall with 8dp corners: the day (Today, Tomorrow, the weekend); Time; the Block (a Block chip, No block, then Other or Choose Block). More options is a plain text row under them with a chevron; it unfolds Estimate, Repeat and Must chips and the details, and its label names what is set ("30 min, Must"). Its title wraps (a new line opens below the first, which stays put, 150ms), and what it read from the words (a day, a #Block) is highlighted in the title in the chips' selected colour and marked on its chip, once; its choices unfold under their chip (grid rows 0fr to 1fr). A task opened from the Inbox shows a back arrow before its title. There is no Save button.
- **Habit editors** (Edit water, Edit <meal>, Edit routine) use the task sheet's row grammar: an icon column, then a stepper (48dp round buttons around a display-small number; holding repeats every 150ms), chips, a switch, or a time button that opens the phone's picker. A switch that turns reminders off dims the rows it governs to 38%. `Habits` (Today ⋮) lists routines, water and the meal times in 56dp rows; meals carry a `Show <meal>` switch.
- **Glass** (the tab bar and Today's habit circle): the fill is the float surface at 100% minus the Settings transparency (Solid to 60%, 20% by default), under a 12px blur with 1.14 saturation in light and 16px with 1.08 in dark (24px and 1.0 above 30%), with a 1px border of black 8% in light and white 8% in dark.

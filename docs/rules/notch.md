# Notch rules and what enforces them

Rules for the notch pill and island (`IslandView`, `IslandWindow`, `NotchScene`, `notch-actions.html`). AGENTS.md points here. Add a row when a correction becomes a check.

| Rule | Enforced by |
|---|---|
| The notch pill ends at the status bar's bottom edge, 4dp above the camera hole, and keeps the cutout's whole width clear, so a wide notch is never drawn over. | `NotchAnchorTest.java` (`aWideNotchKeepsItsWholeWidthClear`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` |
| When the planner store cannot be read, the notch keeps what it showed instead of hiding. | `NotchFrameTest.java` (`anUnreadableStoreKeepsWhatTheIslandShowedInsteadOfHidingIt`) |
| The notch redraws only when what it shows changes: the same frame twice means no redraw, no animation and no window relayout. | `IslandWindowTest.java`, `NotchFrameTest.java` |
| The notch's drawing window never moves: it sits at the screen's top-left at full width and only its height changes, so a morph is never drawn at a stale position. A small touch window over the pill takes its taps. | `IslandWindowTest.java` (`theDrawingWindowNeverMovesOnlyItsHeightChanges`) |
| While the island opens or closes, each face scales with it from its top centre by the tighter of its width and height, so no word or row crosses the island's edge; a settled face is drawn at its own size. Opening, the island widens in the first half; closing, it narrows in the second, so its edge passes the status bar's clock in a few frames. Same on the phone and in the copy. | `IslandShapeTest.java` (`facesShrinkWithTheIslandSoTheyAlwaysFitInsideIt`, `openingWidensFirstAndClosingNarrowsLastSoTheEdgePassesTheClockQuickly`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`faces shrink with the island ...`, `opening widens first ...`) |
| A changing digit rolls a full line inside its own one-em line box, so no glyph drops below the baseline mid-roll. | `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`a changing digit rolls a full line ...`) |
| Only a touch opens the island. Whatever hides it (lock, sleep, turning the phone sideways) closes it and its alert controls, so it comes back as the pill. | `IslandStateTest.java` (`lockingSleepingOrTurningThePhoneSidewaysClosesTheIsland`, `theControllerClosesTheIslandWheneverThePhoneHidesIt`) |
| A tap anywhere on the notch pill opens the island; a finger that drifts less than 8dp is still a tap. | `IslandGestureTest.java` |
| The open, alert and receipt islands have their top at the pill's top and sit as far in from each side as that top is below the screen's top (4 to 16dp), so the three outer margins match. The last row's 40dp control and the last pill end 16dp above the island's bottom. | `IslandShapeTest.java` (`openIslandSitsAsFarInFromEachSideAsItsTopIsBelowTheScreensTop`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`outer margins match ...`) |
| Every open, alert and receipt island has one 36dp corner and a 16dp margin on all four sides: the band's 40dp circle sits at (16, 16), its value ends 16dp from the edge, and the last element ends 16dp above the bottom. Every inner corner is 36 less its inset (20dp for the circle, the 40dp buttons, Stop and Undo); rows are 44dp and fully round; every 40dp control has a 48dp touch area. The playground copy uses the same numbers. | `IslandShapeTest.java` (`marginsAreSixteenDpOnAllFourSides`, `innerRadiusIsOuterMinusPadding`, `thePillsRingIsConcentricWithItsEnds`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`margins are 16dp ...`, `NotchGeometry.java and notch-geometry.mjs match`) |
| The pill's ring and value are the island's: on open they travel into the band beside the camera (ring 28dp to 40dp, value 15sp to 22sp). The ring fills by time used, glasses or steps, and stays empty for an upcoming task. The pill drops AM/PM within 12 hours and is at least 112dp wide. | `NotchFrameTest.java` (`bandBesideCameraHoldsRingAndValue`, `sharedRingAndValueTravelOnOpen`), `NotchSceneTest.java` (`ringProgressPerKind`, `pillValueDropsAmPmWithinTwelveHours`), `IslandShapeTest.java` (`pillMinWidthIs112`) |
| The open island has one primary control (Done, Glass, or Later for a routine), with Later beside Glass when water is due; a control that cannot act is left out, never greyed. Alert islands have two equal pills, Done first. | `NotchSceneTest.java` (`heroHasOnePrimaryControlNeverDisabled`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` |
| A face too tall for 60% of the screen drops rows 3, 2, 1 (open island only), then cuts its title to two lines; nothing is ever clipped: a face that still does not fit makes the island taller. | `IslandShapeTest.java` (`aFaceTooTallForTheScreenDropsRowsThenShortensItsTitleAndIsNeverCut`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`a face too tall for the screen ...`) |
| Every control, row, Undo and the open island's words shrink to 0.97 over 100 ms while pressed (the pill too), on the phone and in the copy; with animations off they snap. | `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`every control shrinks to the same scale while pressed ...`) |
| Every row ends in one control, a quiet 40dp circle (larger at large text, see below; clear, 1dp white 12% ring) 16dp from the island's edge, so its 20dp corner is concentric: a check on a task, a plus on water and a meal, Later on a routine, a chevron on the hero's own alert. A calendar event has none. Water's dots show only at normal text. | `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`every row ends in one quiet 40dp circle ...`) |
| A row title is cut to 1 line (2 at large text, 3 at max) on the phone and in the copy, so the copy's island is as tall as the phone's. | `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`a row title keeps the phone's line count in the copy ...`) |
| A floating island (open, alert, receipt, controls) has a 1dp white 8% edge just inside its outline, fading in as it leaves the pill, so a black or near-black wallpaper cannot swallow it; the pill has none and sits in the status bar like the camera. With nothing due the phone shows no notch at all, only the camera. | `IslandShapeTest.java` (`aFloatingIslandHasAOneDpWhiteEightPercentEdge...`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`a floating island has the phone's 1dp white 8% edge ...`) |
| At large text icons grow with the text up to 1.5 times; pills keep 8dp above and below their label, so they grow taller instead of filling edge to edge; a row's control grows to its icon plus 10dp each side (never under 40dp, its corner stays 20dp). When the island leaves items out (rows dropped to fit, or more than three today), a quiet "+N more" line sits under the rows; it only speaks. The next alert shows once. | `NotchSceneTest.java` (`theIslandSaysHowManyOfTodaysItemsItLeavesOut`, `iconsGrowWithTheTextUpToOneAndAHalfTimes`), `NotchAlertRowsTest.java` (`theNextAlertShowsOnceEvenWhenTheRowsHaveRoomForItTwice`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`large text: icons grow up to 1.5x ...`) |
| Every pill control and Undo is drawn by `NotchParts.pillShape`, which sets the inset background before the padding, so Undo keeps its 18dp ends instead of shrinking to its word. | `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`Undo and every pill keep their side padding ...`), `verify-rpm-glass` receipt drive |
| Only the digits that change roll. With Reduce Motion the island's size and the ring snap, and faces and digits cross-fade over 150 ms. | `NotchFrameTest.java` (`rollingDigitsMoveOnlyChangedDigits`, `reducedMotionFadesInsteadOfMoves`) |
| On the notch the task accent is `#A8C7FA`; a primary control is filled with its accent and labelled in the accent 22% into black (the alarm in `#601410`), so it is the loudest thing on the island. The planner keeps its own blue. | `NotchFrameTest.java` (`primaryControlsAreTheirAccentThirtyPercentIntoBlack`) |
| The island can always log water: with no task to tick its first control is Glass; otherwise due water is one of its rows, rows that must drop to fit keep it (the hero's own alert row drops first), and tapping the row logs a glass in place. | `NotchSceneTest.java` (`noTaskLeftAndWaterDueMakesWaterTheHero`, `rowsAreNextItemThenDueWaterThenNudgesAtMostThree`, `islandAlwaysOffersGlass`), `NotchAlertRowsTest.java` (`theHerosOwnAlertRowNeverPushesDueWaterOutOfTheIsland`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs`, `verify-rpm-glass` `notch-glass` drive |
| Glass, Done and alert changes from the notch save in place through the hidden planner page (`notch-actions.html`, `editPlan`, the planner's own ops), with the version check and one retry; only the meal row, Open, the open island's title and line, and the long press open the planner. An open planner reloads instead of overwriting the change. | `android-companion/notch-ops.test.mjs` (`only the meal row, Open, the open island's words and the long press ...`), `android-companion/notch-actions.test.mjs`, `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`the open island's title and line open the task ...`) |
| After an in-place change the island shows a receipt at once; Undo appears once the change is saved and lasts 5 seconds, its lighter part draining from end to start so the time left shows (it reads the clock, so it counts with animations off), and refuses if anything else changed the plan since. | `NotchAlertRowsTest.java` (`aReceiptShowsAtOnceAndOffersUndoOnlyOnceSaved`), `android-companion/notch-ops.test.mjs` (`Undo from the notch refuses ...`), `NotchAlertRowsTest.java` (`undoShowsItsFiveSecondsDrainingFromTheMomentTheChangeIsSaved`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`Undo shows its five seconds draining ...`) |
| The open island shows the next reminder or alarm still to come today: as the hero's own alert row, as a chip and a Done circle on its task row, or else as a row inside the 3-row cap that keeps due water second. Tapping it opens the alert controls: Turn off as the one primary, then rows to make it a reminder or a ringing alarm, Done and Open. | `NotchAlertRowsTest.java`, `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` |
| A firing reminder offers Done, Snooze 10 min and Open (tap its words); a ringing alarm offers Stop as its full-width primary, with Snooze 10 min and Done as a half-width pair under it, and its time in the band. Done stops the ring and ticks the occurrence it was for; its Undo brings the task back and the ring stays stopped. | `android-companion/notch-ops.test.mjs` (`Done on an alert that fired late ...`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`a ringing alarm puts Stop across the width ...`), `verify-rpm-glass` notch alert drive |
| The time shows once, in the band. The line under the title is the task's purpose, else its block, else Now or Next; water says Due now or Not due yet; an alert says Reminder, Ringing or Ringing alarm. A screen reader still hears the time. | `NotchSceneTest.java` (`theTimeShowsOnceTheLineIsThePurposeElseTheBlockElseNowOrNext`), `NotchAlertRowsTest.java`, `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` (`the time shows once ...`) |
| While the notch can show a ringing alarm (on, unlocked, screen on, upright), the alarm's notification sits in the shade on a quiet channel with no pop-up and no full-screen intent; the alarm still rings. Locked, asleep, sideways or with the notch off, it is posted again as a ringing alarm with its full-screen screen. | `AlarmHeadsUpTest.java` |
| A notch reminder never says "alarm" or "ringing" and never shows the alarm clock; only a ringing alarm does. | `NotchFrameTest.java` (`reminderNeverSaysAlarm`), `playground/notch-scene.test.mjs` |

# Apple's principles of great design (HIG, reintroduced at WWDC26, 8 June 2026)

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles and the WWDC26 session "Principles of great design" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/250/). Quoted from the session summaries.

1. **Purpose — make something meaningful.** "Designing experiences that genuinely serve people: ones that respect and adapt to their lives, are clear and considered." Every feature asks for time, attention and trust; decide what not to build.
2. **Agency — let people do things their own way.** "Putting people in control... avoid forcing them down predetermined paths, and build in forgiveness: easy undo and confirmation for destructive actions."
3. **Responsibility — act in people's best interest.** Ask only for what is necessary; anticipate harm, "especially with AI".
4. **Familiarity — build on what people know.** Follow established conventions for common actions; "things that look the same should work the same."
5. **Flexibility — adapt to diverse contexts and needs.** Different contexts (on the go, one hand), abilities (large text, reduced motion) and personal preference.
6. **Simplicity — be clear and direct.** "Simplicity isn't minimalism: it's removing friction. Concise language, strong visual hierarchy, and clear affordances... Every element should earn its place, and sometimes adding context makes an interface simpler."
7. **Craft — care about every detail.** "Beautiful typography, responsive animations, solid performance, and a commitment to iteration."
8. **Delight — make it human.** "Delight isn't confetti: it's the natural result of getting everything else right. Identify the emotion you want people to feel, then reinforce it."

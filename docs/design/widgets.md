# Home-screen widgets

The widget is a RemoteViews bar, so it uses native resources instead of theme.css. `res/values/colors.xml` and `res/values-night/colors.xml` mirror the theme.css roles as `rpm_*` colours; change both files together. The bar is `surface-container` with the launcher's widget corner radius on Android 12+ (28dp before). From the start: the app icon (32dp in a 48dp target, M3 pressed state) opens the planner; then a 48dp full pill in `surface-container-highest` reads "Capture a thought" (16sp, `on-surface-variant`, one line) and holds, at its end, a 40dp round mic button in `primary-container`. The pill opens Capture ready to type; the mic opens it listening. Custom fonts are not available in RemoteViews, so the widget uses the system sans-serif.

The second widget, Next up (2 × 2, `NextUpWidget`, `res/layout/widget_next_up.xml`; the approved capture bar look is `approved-home-widget.png`), is a `surface-container` square with 12dp padding. Top row: the next task's 40dp tone capsule with its `task-symbol.mjs` icon (the area colour, `outline` without one) and, at the end, a 36dp countdown ring in the same tone that fills over the last hour before the start, or by time used once it runs. Under them: "in 50 min" (12sp medium, `primary`; "at 4:30 PM" further away, "Now · 20 min left" while it runs), then the title (15sp medium, `on-surface`, up to 2 lines). Bottom row: the planner's segmented water meter (bitmap, 40dp), a 44dp mic in `surface-container-highest` and a 44dp `primary` plus. The art is drawn twice, light and dark, and `values-night` styles pick one, so a theme change needs no redraw. Below 156dp wide (two cells on a five-column home screen) the compact layout uses 8dp padding and 36dp controls. Text follows the Settings widget text size.

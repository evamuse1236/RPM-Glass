# Capture ramble baseline, 19 October 2026

The baseline passed **36/50 cases, 72%**. The ten hardest cases passed **11/30 repeated runs, 36.7%**. Together that is 47/80, 58.8%, with the hardest cases deliberately overrepresented. This measures the live **CLI transport** plus the production Capture backend, not the phone API.

The corpus has 50 cases, 11 multi-turn cases, and two monologues of 624 and 643 words. Each run used a separate seeded store and concurrency 3. The fixture reused rpmctl's gauntlet seed generator, then supplied six stable tasks, a read-only dentist calendar event, two drafts, water and meals. The clock was Monday 19 October 2026 at 10 am IST.

Classes overlap and count affected case-runs. A failed early turn remains a failure even if a later turn recovers. Focus checks inspect the effective persisted draft, preserve every surviving proposal's fields, and catch extra proposals. Saved-item checks use canonical rows; a proposal alone never counts as a saved task.

The hardest-case results were consistent for draft removal and the nine-item ramble, which each failed 3/3. Both monologues failed 3/3. Ambiguous gym, gym disambiguation and Result/Purpose/Must each passed 3/3. The Hinglish tomorrow-versus-yesterday case passed 2/3.

# Does Capture change the plan by itself, or ask first?

Type: wayfinder:grilling (HITL) · Stream: capture · Status: closed · Assignee: you · Blocked by: none

## Question

Capture works out what a ramble means: a new task, a reminder, a project, a time change, an edit to an earlier task, or a question. PRODUCT.md said planning changes need review first. Which changes may it make on its own?

## Resolution (user, 2026-10-18)

Capture acts on its own. User's words: "generally just do it by yourself, for some things change and then just confirm".

How we read it (inferred; correct it if wrong):
- It always saves your exact words first.
- New tasks, reminders, projects and answers apply straight away. Capture says what it did ("Added: call bank, 10:30"), with Undo.
- Changes to things that already exist (moving, editing, deleting) also apply straight away, then show a one-tap card: Keep or Undo. Deleted items go to Trash, so nothing is lost.
- Every change is logged with your words, what it decided and whether you pressed Undo. An Undo marks the capture as a likely mistake for review.

Replaces the PRODUCT.md rule that planning changes need review before they apply.

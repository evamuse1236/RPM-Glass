# Notch break scenarios, 2026-10-20

Found by reading the code on `gauntlet/notch` (base `d879b7d`) and by shooting the playground copy (`shoot-notch.mjs`, round 0 in `notch-shots/round-0/`). Paths are under `app/src/main/java/com/rpm/prototype/` unless they say otherwise.

Severity: **breaks** (the person loses an action or sees wrong state), **ugly** (works, looks or feels wrong), **fine** (handled; evidence given). Status after the fixes is in `notch-report.md`.

| # | Scenario | What happens now | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Island open, then the phone locks, sleeps or turns sideways | The island hides, but `expanded` and the alert controls stay set. When the phone is unlocked or turned back, the open island (or the alert controls) comes back over the app without a tap. | breaks | `NotchController.java:221` hides without clearing `expanded` or `managed`; only `setExpanded` and alerts clear them |
| 2 | Large or max text with the hero's own alert and due water | The panel builder shrinks to 1 row when the face is too tall. The hero's alert row takes that row (`room--`), then `visibleRows(0)` drops the water row too, so the island cannot log a glass. Breaks the rule "the island can always log water". | breaks | `NotchContent.java:120-128`, `NotchScene.java:211` (`n == 0` drops water) |
| 3 | A face taller than 60% of the screen (alert controls, alerts, receipt at max text with a long title) | Only the open island drops rows. Every other face is cut at the cap, so the last rows are clipped: at 200% text the alert controls lose `Open`. | breaks | `NotchPill.java:118-122`; round 0 `13-max-text-manage.png` (face 527dp, island 499dp) |
| 4 | Wide or tall notch cutout (not a punch hole) | The hole is taken as `min(width, height)`, so on a notch 80dp wide and 30dp tall the pill keeps only ~42dp clear and draws its ring and value under the notch. | breaks (other phones) | `NotchAnchor.java:33`, `NotchPill.java:101` |
| 5 | The store cannot be read for a moment (a write in progress, a bad byte) | `NotchData.read` returns `EMPTY`, so the pill, or an island the person is using, disappears until the next save. | ugly | `NotchData.java:42-47` |
| 6 | Open island: the person wants the task itself | Only a long press on the pill opens the task. In the open island the title and line do nothing on tap, unlike the alert island's words. | ugly (slower to act) | `NotchContent.panel`; `NotchController.onGesture` OPEN handles only UP |
| 7 | Pressing a control | Java controls change only their fill. The playground copy also scales them to 0.97, so the copy and the phone differ and the phone's press is faint on a dark fill. | ugly | `NotchParts.pill`, `playground/notch.css:202-203` |
| 8 | Alert row's Done circle | The circle ends 10dp from the island edge while every other row value ends at 16dp, so it looks pushed out of the column (and its corner is not concentric at that inset). | ugly | `NotchContent.java:149`, `playground/notch.css:155`; round 0 `02-open.png` |
| 9 | Long or non-Latin row titles in the copy | Java cuts a row title to one line (two at large text). The copy's row title has no clamp and wraps to as many lines as it needs, so the playground shows a taller island than the phone. | ugly (copy drift) | `NotchParts.row` (`wrapping(..., 1)`), `playground/notch.css:133`; round 0 `09-long-title.png` (Hindi row on two lines) |
| 10 | No tasks or empty day | Nothing is due: the pill hides. With due water and no task, water is the hero with Glass. | fine | `NotchController.java:220-224`; `NotchSceneTest.noTaskLeftAndWaterDueMakesWaterTheHero`; round 0 `07-empty-day.png`, `08-water-hero.png` |
| 11 | Very long task title | The island title wraps whole words to 2 lines (3 large, 4 max) and ends with an ellipsis; the pill shows only the time. | fine | `NotchParts.title`; round 0 `09-long-title.png`, `10-long-title-max-text.png` |
| 12 | Many due items at once | Rows cap at 3, the next alert leads, due water stays second; the second kind sits in the split circle. | fine | `NotchSceneTest.rowsAreNextItemThenDueWaterThenNudgesAtMostThree`; round 0 `12-many-due.png` |
| 13 | An alarm and a reminder at the same time | The ringing alarm wins; a reminder that fires during it does not replace it. The reminder's own notification is already posted. | fine | `NotchAlerting.java:86`, `CompanionAlerts.java:86` |
| 14 | Landscape, lock screen, always-on display | The island hides. A ringing alarm keeps its full-screen notification with Snooze and Dismiss. | fine (but see #1) | `NotchController.java:221`, `CompanionAlarmService.java:33-34` |
| 15 | Full-screen video or immersive apps (portrait) | The pill stays over the content around the camera, as Apple's island does. Sideways video hides it. | fine | `NotchController.java:221` (landscape only) |
| 16 | Keyboard open | The island sits at the top and takes no text, so the keyboard never covers it. | fine | `IslandShape.island` (top at the pill's top) |
| 17 | Split screen and pop-up windows | The overlay sizes from the whole display (`getMaximumWindowMetrics`), not the app window. | fine | `NotchPill.displayWidth`, `displayHeight` |
| 18 | Dark and light wallpaper | The island is black with a shadow; on black it blends in like a camera surround. Text is white or a tested accent on black. | fine | round 0 `11-dark.png`; `NotchFrameTest.primaryControlsAreTheirAccentThirtyPercentIntoBlack` |
| 19 | App process killed and restarted | Android rebinds the accessibility service; `attach` re-reads the store and asks the alarm service what rings. A receipt and its Undo are lost. | fine | `NotchAccessibilityService.onServiceConnected`, `NotchAlerting.attach` |
| 20 | Overlay permission revoked / service turned off | The notch uses `TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY`, which needs no overlay permission; turning the service off unbinds and `detach` removes both windows. | fine | `NotchController.attach`, `detach` |

| 23 | Midnight rollover and time-zone change | The minute tick, `TIME_CHANGED` and `TIMEZONE_CHANGED` redraw from the clock; today's slots are rebuilt from `now` each time and a task across midnight still overlaps today. | fine | `NotchController.attach` filter, `NotchDay.today` |
| 24 | Camera hole off centre, in a corner, or no cutout | Off centre: the pill centres on the hole. No cutout: a 36dp pill top-centre, 12dp down. A corner hole: the pill is pushed on screen and its ring can sit under the hole. | ugly (corner only; not the S24 FE) | `IslandShape.java:49`, `NotchAnchorTest.noCutoutFallsBackToTopCentreTwelveDpDown` |
| 25 | Touch near the top edge, drags that are not taps | The pill's touch box starts at the screen's top edge, so a pull down that starts on the pill opens the island instead of the shade (the shade still pulls from anywhere else). A drift under 8dp is a tap; a drag short of 90dp (sideways) or 48dp (down) does nothing. | fine (deliberate: pull down opens) | `IslandShape.java:54`, `IslandGestureTest` |
| 26 | Battery saver and Reduce Motion | Both turn animators off; sizes snap and faces cross-fade over 150 ms. | fine | `NotchFrameTest.reducedMotionFadesInsteadOfMoves` |

The open island's face is rebuilt once a minute when its line changes ("24 min left"), which drops a press that is down at that moment. | fine (rare press loss: known) | `IslandWindowTest`, `NotchFrameTest.windowEventsWithinTheSameMinuteDoNotChangeThePill` |

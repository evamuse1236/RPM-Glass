## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG sprite for an Android floating assistant button and app icon. Primary request: one beautiful, immediately recognizable butterfly in crisp pixel-art game style, inspired by a lavender, pink and midnight-blue butterfly phone wallpaper. Subject: a single symmetrical butterfly with open wings, small dark indigo body, lavender outer wings, pink inner wing pixels, a few cream-white pixel highlights. Style: carefully drawn 16-bit sprite, a coherent coarse square pixel grid and sharp stair-stepped silhouette, flat limited palette with discrete pixel shading; no smooth gradients, no blurred glow, no anti-aliased curves. Composition: single centered butterfly fills about 75 percent of square canvas, complete antennae and wings visible, generous transparent margin. Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no checkerboard drawn into the pixels. Text: none. Constraints: no frame, no button circle, no UI, no device, no scenery, no person, no additional butterflies, no watermark. The silhouette must remain legible as a small 40dp launcher. Deliver one final project asset.
